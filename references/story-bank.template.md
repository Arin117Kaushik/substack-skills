# Story Bank

What you actually have to say: the facts, numbers, moments and opinions every writing skill in this bundle is allowed to use. Built by `substack-interviewer`. Nothing goes in here that you didn't say.

**Where it lives:** `~/.substack-skills/story-bank.md`. The interviewer copies this template there on first run. It stays out of the plugin folder (updates would wipe it) and out of any git repo (a public fork would publish it).

## Status

- filled: no
- updated: --
- thin sections: all

## 1. Who you are

Roles, credentials and background, only as you stated them.

- (e.g. "Ran ops at a 12-person agency for 3 years", said 2026-09-16)

## 2. Numbers with a referent

Each number with what it counts and when.

- (e.g. "Job search went from about 4 hours a day to 40 minutes, Aug 2026")

## 3. Turning points

Dated moments where something changed.

- (e.g. "Agency folded the team, August 2026")

## 4. Scars

Things that failed, cost you, or embarrassed you, and what you learned.

-

## 5. Positions

Opinions someone could reasonably argue with.

- (e.g. "Automate the search, never the voice")

## 6. Things you made

Projects, tools, posts, products, with a link if public.

-

## 7. Off limits

Topics, names, employers or details never to mention.
